About the Book

A testimony of faith forged in the deepest valley a parent can walk

When Lylianne and Hunter Thompson lost their seventeen-month-old son Remington, their world stopped turning. Remembering Remington is the honest, deeply personal account of how grief, faith, and the promises of God met them in their darkest moment — and carried them through.

This is not merely a grief memoir. It is a testament to the sustaining power of Jesus Christ — a young couple choosing trust when bitterness would have been easier, and discovering that even the deepest pain can become a place where God's nearness is known.

If you are hurting, this book will steady you. If you are doubting, it will anchor you. If you are searching, it will point you to Christ.

"Grief didn't take our faith away — it forced us to decide what it was built on."

— Lylianne Vaughn Thompson

About the Author

Lylianne Vaughn Thompson

with Hunter Thompson & Jerry Drummonds

Lylianne Thompson is a wife, mother, and woman of deep faith who walked through the unimaginable loss of her son Remington at seventeen months old. Her story is one of grief surrendered — not suppressed — and of a God who remained faithful in the silence.

Written alongside her husband Hunter and her great-grandfather, the Reverend Jerry Drummonds, this book is a family's testimony: that love does not end, that Heaven is real, and that one day every tear will be wiped away.
